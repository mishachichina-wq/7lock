7lock — iOS 8 visual update (source, not compiled)

- HelveticaNeue-UltraLight 78pt clock (70pt iPad fallback), adjusted time/date layout.
- Date language/format remains unchanged.
- Camera asset replaced with a smaller iOS 8-style icon; original gesture view retained.
- Slide Text is again loaded from tweak preferences; one chevron is prepended by GlintySlider.
